# Privacy Policy for Gameplay Recorder

**Effective date:** 16 November 2026

This policy explains how the Android app **Gameplay Recorder** (package `com.gameplayrecorder.mufflerexoz`, "the app") handles your information.

## Summary

- The app does **not** collect, store on servers, sell or share personal data.
- Your recordings are saved **only on your device**.
- There are **no ads, no analytics, no tracking** and no account.
- Purchases are handled entirely by **Google Play**.

## Information the app accesses

### Screen content
When you start a recording and accept Android's screen capture prompt, the app captures what is shown on your screen. This happens only while a recording is running, which Android shows with a notification and a status bar indicator.

### Audio
If you turn on **Internal audio** or **Microphone**, the app uses the Record Audio permission to capture:
- sound played by other apps (games, music, videos) that allow it, and/or
- your microphone.

Audio is captured only during a recording and is written into the video file.

### Recordings
Videos are saved as MP4 files in **Movies/GameplayRecorder** on your device. The app never uploads them. They stay under your control: you can view, share or delete them with your gallery or file manager. Uninstalling the app does not delete videos you have saved.

### Settings
Your choices (resolution, frame rate, audio source and similar) are stored locally on your device and are not sent anywhere.

## Purchases

Gameplay Recorder is unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase through **Google Play Billing**. Google processes the payment and your payment details. The app never sees your card or payment information. It only receives a confirmation from Google Play that the purchase was made, and saves an "unlocked" flag on your device. Google's handling of your data is described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Promo codes are redeemed in the Google Play Store app, not in Gameplay Recorder.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Record audio | Internal audio and microphone recording, only while recording |
| Foreground service (media projection, microphone) | Keep recording while you play another app |
| Post notifications | Show the recording notification with the Stop button |
| Google Play billing | One-time purchase to unlock the app |

## Data sharing

The app does not share any data with third parties. The only network communication is between the app and Google Play to check and complete your purchase.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Security

Because recordings and settings never leave your device, their security depends on your device's own protections (screen lock, encryption). Videos you choose to share are handled by the app you share them with.
