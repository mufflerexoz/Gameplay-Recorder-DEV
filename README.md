## Features

- 🎮 **Record your screen**: games, apps and the home screen.
- 🔊 **Internal audio**: capture the sound from games, music and videos.
- 🎙️ **Microphone**: add your commentary.
- 🎚️ **Mix both**: game sound and your voice in one video.
- 📺 **720p or 1080p**, at **30 or 60 fps**.
- 💾 **Small files, high quality**: H.264 that plays everywhere, or H.265 for about 35% smaller files.
- ⚙️ **Quality presets**: Saver, Balanced or High, with a size estimate per minute.
- 🕹️ **Controller support**: navigate everything with a gamepad, with a green highlight.
- 🖤 **Game overlay style**: dark interface with a simple icon menu.
- 📁 Videos are saved to **Movies/GameplayRecorder** and appear in your Gallery.
- 🔒 **Private**: no ads, no account, no tracking. Your videos never leave your phone.

## Requirements

- Android 10 or newer.
- Some apps block internal audio capture (for example protected video and calls), and Android does not let any recorder capture system sounds. Turn on the microphone to include those.
